Yuki Kanayama

I am a PhD candidate in Economic Geography at the London School of Economics. Before joining LSE, I worked for Japan’s Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism, on regulatory reform in the construction industry and international cooperation on railway projects. I received my B.A. (2018) and M.A. (2020) in Economics from Waseda University, Japan.

My research interests include empirical studies on urban economics, particularly on housing supply constraints. I have been working on housing dereliction in a superstar city (Tokyo) and lot geometry and vacancy persistence (Tokyo). My current projects include housing consumption (England) and voluntary vacancies as a housing supply constraint (Japan).

My supervisors are Christian Hilber and Felipe Carozzi.
